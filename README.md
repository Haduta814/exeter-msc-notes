# MSc AI & Data Science – Shared Notes (University of Exeter, 2025-26)

Lecture slides, workshop notebooks (with solutions where available) and recommended reading from my MSc, organised by module, shared with my PhD cohort.

> **Please keep this repo private.** Lecture slides and workshop materials are the copyright of the University of Exeter and the individual lecturers. They are shared here for the personal study of cohort members only. Please don't redistribute them.

Coursework, assessment briefs, marks and the dissertation are deliberately not included.
